🧠 How to play

No words. Just doodles and movie knowledge.

01   One player secretly gets a Malayalam movie title.
02   Draw objects, scenes, or clues on the canvas.
03   Teammates type guesses before the timer runs out.
04   Correct guess = 100 points for the team.
05   The game ends at the selected movie count.
💡 Pro tip
Think of a movie's most recognisable prop, place, or scene. Don't draw letters or spell the title!
